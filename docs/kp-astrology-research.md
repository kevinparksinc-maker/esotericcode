# KP Astrology Research Notes for EsotericCode

## Design findings

KP astrology places special interpretive emphasis on the degree of each house cusp and its sign lord, nakshatra (star) lord, and sub-lord. The sub-lord is the finer-grained factor within a nakshatra, while significators connect planets to houses. Exact time and location materially affect a traditional KP chart because they change the ascendant and cusp divisions.

For EsotericCode, a public repository has a trustworthy origin timestamp but no meaningful geographic birthplace. The product will therefore present this layer as a **repository symbolic chart**, not a traditional personal natal chart or predictive astrology calculation. Its deterministic correspondences will map repository creation and change-cycle time to KP-inspired planet, house, nakshatra, and sub-lord structures, then explain the resulting engineering themes alongside the existing Tarot and I Ching evidence.
